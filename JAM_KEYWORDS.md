  JAM KEYWORDS  —  what to say to the local model
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Two habits that matter most:
  • Keep it short and literal.  "more bass" beats a long sentence.
  • Name the layer — drums / bass / lead / vox — so it knows
    exactly what to touch (those match the template toggles).


▶  OPEN A SESSION
     Say:   "Let's jam"
     Does:  Greets, checks the bridge on 4321, reads the
            current pattern.


▶  FRESH SONG FROM SCRATCH
     Say:   "Start a new pattern"
            also: "new song" · "create a new pattern"
                  "use the template" · "blank slate"
     Does:  Replaces the file with the standard template
            (header, toggles, postgain sliders, stack).


▶  KEEP MY WORK, JUST FORMAT IT
     Say:   "Upgrade this to the template"
            also: "upgrade to template standard"
     Does:  Wraps your CURRENT layers in the template
            structure — does not wipe them.


▶  CHANGE THE MUSIC
     Say:   "more bass"      "half-time drums"
            "make it darker"  "add a lead"  "drop the hats"
     Does:  Edits the pattern — smallest change, keeps
            your toggles.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  ONE DISTINCTION TO REMEMBER
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  "new pattern" / "use the template"   →  fresh start (blank)
  "upgrade to the template"            →  keep what's playing
